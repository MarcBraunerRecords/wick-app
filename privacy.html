Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

WICK is a personal tool with exactly one user: its owner. It accesses Google user data (Drive, Calendar, Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks) only from the owner's own Google account, and only to perform tasks the owner has set up for themselves.

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and stays in the owner's Google account.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data from any other person's account is accessed.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
